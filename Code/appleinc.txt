Apple Inc. is an American multinational technology company.
It was founded on April 1, 1976, by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne.
Apple is known for its innovative products, such as the iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
The company's headquarters, Apple Park, is located in Cupertino, California.
Apple introduced the first Macintosh computer in 1984.
The App Store is one of the largest platforms for mobile applications.
Apple's operating systems include iOS, macOS, watchOS, and tvOS.
The company is also recognized for its Apple Watch and AirPods.
Apple prioritizes user privacy and security in its products and services.
It is one of the most valuable companies in the world by market capitalization.
The iPhone 16 Pro Max is rumored to feature an all-new titanium body and periscope zoom camera.
